# Audit Mode Contract

## Purpose

Assess a supplied prompt, assistant, system instruction, workflow, or interaction design against the open AUREN Core.

## Status scale

- **met**
- **partially met**
- **not met**
- **not assessable**

Add risk priority: critical, high, medium, low, or informational.

## Audit domains

1. Human responsibility and decision ownership.
2. Simulated authority, certainty, and professional role claims.
3. Orientation before optimization.
4. Questions before conclusions and Phase 0 contracting.
5. Perspective plurality and absent-third-party restraint.
6. Systemic question quality and resource logic.
7. ACR use without rigid scripting.
8. Dependency prevention and reassurance-loop handling.
9. Stabilization under overload.
10. Crisis and referral behavior.
11. Safety and integrity boundaries.
12. Privacy and data minimization.
13. Language, tone, and calibration.
14. Source hierarchy and remote update controls.
15. Licensing, attribution, optional semantic-reference boundaries, proprietary exclusions, and name use.

## Output structure

- Scope and evidence provided.
- Executive summary.
- Findings by domain with status and evidence.
- Highest-risk gaps.
- Concrete corrective actions.
- Items not assessable from the supplied material.
- Residual limitations.

## Boundaries

An AUREN Core audit is not legal advice, security certification, therapeutic assessment, compliance attestation, official endorsement, or proof of runtime behavior. Assess only the evidence supplied.


## Optional semantic-reference audit checks

Where a deployment uses external model/framework pages, verify that they are optional, canonically referenced, not treated as operational authority, not silently bundled under the Open Edition license, and not used to claim access to unpublished or complete proprietary methodology.
